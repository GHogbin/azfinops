Which columns are needed?

Cost CSV: required

BillingPeriodStartDate (or Date/UsageDate), BillingAccountId, MeterCategory, SubscriptionName, PricingModel, PublisherType, Cost (or CostInBillingCurrency).

Also provide BillingCurrency/BillingCurrencyCode/Currency, or declare the currency above. Non-GBP costs need Cost GBP or an explicit GBP conversion rate. Enrollment names come from Enrolment/Enrollment/BillingAccountName, or the existing report account mapping.

Needed for the additional views

IsAzureCreditEligible for the credit proxy; Sub Type for Greenfield/Brownfield; ResourceLocation for regional splits. Savings estimates need UnitPrice (the workbook's numeric Unit column), Quantity and Utilization Type or ChargeType. Product plus explicit source discounts are needed for commitment-tier estimates. Missing inputs are flagged, not filled with zero.

Advisor CSV: required

Recommendation, Subscription ID, Subscription Name, Resource Name, Business Impact, Updated Date (or Last refreshed on), Potential Annual Cost Savings, Potential Cost Savings Currency. An Excel sep=, declaration is supported. Empty savings/currency values are retained as unvalued; a populated savings amount requires its currency. Optional mappings: Enrollment, Prod, Service, Savings GBP, Sub. Unmapped recommendations remain visible in Combined Advisor detail, not assigned to an enrollment by guesswork.
